lodecks is **free and open source software** — built in the open, for
everyone who wants to play with music and code. It's licensed under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html)
(AGPL-3.0-or-later).

## What that means

- **You can use it** for anything, including performing, teaching and
  commercial work.
- **You can study and change it.** The source is the whole program.
- **You can share it**, changed or not, under the same license.
- **If you run a changed version for others over a network**, for example a
  hosted lodecks, you must offer those users its source code. This is what
  the "Affero" part adds to the GPL.

There is no warranty: lodecks is provided as is. The full terms are in the
license.

## Why the AGPL

lodecks is built on [Strudel](https://strudel.cc) and
[Hydra](https://hydra.ojack.xyz), both AGPL. The AGPL keeps lodecks, and what
others build from it, open in the same way.

## Music is not covered

The AGPL covers lodecks' code and its demo packs. It says nothing about the
songs you load: a pack made from a song still holds that song's audio, which
belongs to whoever owns the song. See the [terms](/docs/about/terms).
