---
title: "Privacy"
description: "What lodecks keeps, where, and what it sends."
---

lodecks runs in your browser and has **no accounts, no analytics and no
tracking**.

## What stays on your machine

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Your sets, their history, your effects and gifs | the browser's storage (IndexedDB) |
| Your packs: downloaded, imported or saved | the browser's storage (IndexedDB) |
| The stem model, once downloaded | the browser's cache |
| Layout choices (open tabs, split size…) | the browser's local storage |

Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything kept there.

## What leaves your machine

- **The stem model**: the first time you import a song, lodecks asks, then
  downloads the model from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co). Like any
  download, that request is seen by Hugging Face.
- **Packs you download**: fetched from the lodecks server. Nothing goes back
  the other way: saving is always local.
- **Gifs from a url**: the lodecks server fetches the url you give it.

Songs you import are **not uploaded**: splitting and analysing them happens in
your browser.

## The camera

`camera()` asks the browser for the camera first. The picture is only used
for the visuals, on your machine; it is not recorded or sent.
