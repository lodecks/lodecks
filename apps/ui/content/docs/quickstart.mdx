## Open it

Head to [lodecks.com/play](https://lodecks.com/play). That's it — no install,
no setup, no account. lodecks runs right in your browser.

<Callout>
  Browsers stay silent until you interact with the page — click anywhere or
  press a key, and you're good to go.
</Callout>

## A first set

Your set is code, split into blocks by blank lines. Put the cursor in a
block, hit **⌘↵** (Ctrl+Enter on Windows and Linux), and it runs.

Every example on these pages is live — press **play** or ⌘↵ right inside
the code. Tweak it, run it again, break it, fix it. One plays at a time, and
`a` is always the pack **intro**.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Drop a track on a deck

<Try code={`const a = deck("a").load("intro").show()`} />

`deck("a")` grabs deck A, `.load()` puts a pack on it, and `.show()` draws the deck right under your
line. Run it again and nothing reloads — the song just keeps going.
</Step>

<Step>
### Hit play

<Try code={`a.play()
xfade("a")`} />

`play()` starts the clock and brings the song in on the next phrase. You're
live.
</Step>

<Step>
### Jump with cues

**Hot cues** are bookmarks in the song — up to 8 per pack. intro's take you to
the intro, verse, drop, verse 2, drop 2… `cue(n)` jumps there, right on the
beat.

<Try code={`a.play()
a.show("cues")
next(() => a.cue(3), 4)   // to the drop, after 4 bars`} />

The cue pads under the line work too: click one mid-song and you're there.
Shift+click drops a new cue wherever the playhead is.
</Step>

<Step>
### Get your hands on it

<Try code={`a.play()
a.filter(slider(0, -1, 1))
a.eq("low", knob(0, -1, 0.5))`} />

A slider and a knob pop up right inside the code. Drag them and the numbers
in the code follow — your set always says exactly what you hear.
</Step>

<Step>
### Blend into B

<Try code={`a.play()
xfade("a")
b.tune()       // B glides into A's key
b.play()
xfade("b", 8)  // crossfade over 8 bars`} />

Two songs, one key, a smooth 8-bar blend. (Here `b` is a small demo pack.)

Want lodecks to pick the perfect moment? `b.take(16)` finds the best point in
both songs and blends into B over 16 bars.
</Step>

<Step>
### Light it up

<Try code={`a.play()
layers(viz("tunnel"), viz("plasma").blend("add").opacity(kick())).show()`} />

A tunnel, with plasma flashing over it on every kick.
</Step>
</Steps>

That's a set. **⌘S** saves a snapshot to its history, **⌘. ⌘.** (twice) pulls
the plug on everything, and `help()` brings up the cheat sheet anytime.
