---
title: "The pack editor"
description: "Open up a pack, hear it on its own, and shape it."
---

Lift the hood on a pack. The editor lays the song out as an arrangement — a
ruler with its sections and hot cues, then one lane per layer, right where it
plays. Listen, move cues, rename and trim, then save.

A pack opens in a tab of the editor: ✎ in the packs menu, or **edit** on a
deck. It works on a copy of its own, so what the decks are playing is never
touched, and you can open several packs at once.

## Listening

The editor plays the pack on **its own clock**, at the pack's own tempo,
without starting the main clock or touching the set. It is heard in the
headphones when cueing is on, and on the main output otherwise.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| ▶ / ⏸, or **Space** | play and pause |
| ■ | stop: back to where you pressed play, to try it again |
| click the ruler or a lane | move the playhead there |
| click a layer's name | play from where that layer starts |
| **M** / **S** | mute or solo a layer, in the editor only |

## Cues

Drag a cue along the ruler to move it (it snaps to bars, Shift snaps to
beats). Shift+click the ruler adds a cue, Alt+click a cue removes it, and
double-clicking one renames it.

## Layers

The **⋯** next to each layer:

- **rename** (or double-click its name);
- **star**, to mark the ones you use most;
- **delete layer**, to take it out of the pack.

## Zoom

**+** / **−**, or Ctrl+scroll, zoom around the pointer; **fit** shows the
whole song; **follow** keeps the playhead in view. Lanes are drawn from the
audio at any zoom.

## Saving

**⌘S** (Ctrl+S) or **save** keeps the changes in your browser — always
local, never on the server. Saving a pack you downloaded keeps your own copy;
the original stays on the server. A dot on the tab means there are changes
not saved; closing it asks first.
