---
title: "Import a song"
description: "Drop a track. Make it yours."
---

Got a track you love? Drop it anywhere in lodecks — mp3, wav, flac, ogg,
m4a — or pick **import a song…** from the packs menu. lodecks splits it into
stems, finds the beat and maps the song, right on your own machine. Nothing
gets uploaded.

## What happens

<Steps>
<Step>
### Decode
The file is read and decoded by the browser.
</Step>
<Step>
### Split into stems
A neural network (HT-Demucs) splits the song into drums, bass, other and
vocals. The first time, lodecks asks before downloading the model (about
170 MB, from Hugging Face); after that it is kept in the browser.

It runs on the graphics card through WebGPU when the browser has it, and on
the processor otherwise. A four-minute song takes a minute or two.
</Step>
<Step>
### Analyse
lodecks finds the tempo and where the bars start, the key, the sections, and
cuts the vocals into phrases for the pads.
</Step>
<Step>
### Build
The stems are compressed and everything is packed into a `.lopack`.
</Step>
</Steps>

The import can be cancelled at any point. When it is done, the pack opens in
the [pack editor](/docs/packs/editor): check the cues and names, play it,
then save it.

## Saving

A fresh import isn't kept until you save it — **⌘S** or **save** in the
editor keeps it in your browser, like every pack you save. It is named after
its title on the first save, so rename it first by double-clicking the title.
