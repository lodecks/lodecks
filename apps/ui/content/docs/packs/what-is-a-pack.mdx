---
title: "What is a pack"
description: "Songs, unpacked: everything you need to take a track apart and put it back together."
---

A **pack** is a song that's ready to be played, not just listened to.
Everything lives in one `.lopack` file:

- **Stems**: the song split into layers, usually drums, bass, other and
  vocals. Together they are the song; apart, you can mute and solo them.
- **Voices**: short parts placed on the pads: vocal phrases cut on the bars,
  shouts, risers, impacts. Each is a **loop**, a **oneshot** or a **stem**,
  and has a pad key (1–0).
- **The grid**: tempo, time signature and where bar 1 starts, so everything
  lands on the beat.
- **Key**, **sections** (intro, verse, drop…) and up to 8 **hot cues**.

## Local first

Your packs live **in your browser**. That's where they play from, and that's
where they're saved — lodecks never writes anything back to the server.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| ☁ **on the server** | packs ready to download, like **intro** |
| 🖴 **in this browser** | packs you downloaded, imported or saved |
| ✦ **new** | a fresh import, not saved yet |

Load a pack that's only on the server — `deck("a", "intro")`, or **a** / **b**
in the packs menu — and lodecks downloads it first, then plays it from your
browser; next time it's already there. The packs menu also has a **download**
button, with its progress.

Edit a downloaded pack and save, and you keep **your own copy** in the
browser; the server's stays as it is. Remove your copy from the packs menu
and the server's version is there to download again.

## Getting packs

- Download one from the packs menu.
- [Import a song](/docs/packs/import) right in the browser.
- Drop a `.lopack` file onto lodecks to keep it in your browser.

<Callout title="Your music, your rights">
  A pack made from a song contains that song's audio. Only make, keep and
  share packs of music you have the rights to use. See the
  [terms](/docs/about/terms).
</Callout>
