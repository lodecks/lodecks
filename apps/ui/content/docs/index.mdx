Meet **lodecks**, a live coding DJ playground built for the browser. Mix tracks, remix stems, trigger samples, shape effects, and create visuals that move with your music. All in real time, all in sync.

No traditional DJ workflow. No rigid interface. Just you, your music, and code.

## Your music, your playground

Load **song packs** onto decks A and B and make them your own. Play full tracks, isolate drums, bass, or vocals, chop phrases, launch one-shots, and blend everything together.

Every sound shares the same musical clock. Loops lock in, cues land on beat, and transitions stay tight — even when you're rewriting the music as it plays.

And here's the fun part: **your code is your controller.**

Write a line, hit `⌘↵`, and hear it come alive. Sliders, pads, decks, mixers, and visuals appear right where you need them, directly alongside the code that created them.

No switching between coding and performing. They're the same thing.

Explore [**the editor**](/docs/live/editor).

## What makes it different

- **Songs, unpacked.** Forget juggling audio files. A song pack brings together stems, phrases, one-shots, tempo, key, sections, and cues — everything you need to take a track apart and put it back together. [Explore song packs](/docs/packs/what-is-a-pack).

- **Drop a track. Make it yours.** Import an MP3 and let lodecks separate its stems, find the beat, and map the music — right in your browser, on your own machine. [Import your first song](/docs/packs/import).

- **Always in the groove.** Every pad hit, loop, cue jump, and code change snaps to the shared musical grid. Improvise freely without losing the beat.

- **Songs that never end.** Flip a deck to infinite and it becomes a jukebox that never runs out: it finds the bars that sound alike and keeps branching between them, right on the beat. [Go infinite](/docs/live/infinite).

- **Harmony, handled.** Two tracks, different keys? `a.tune()` smoothly shifts one deck toward the other's key, while the top bar helps you spot harmonic clashes before they happen.

- **Code the sound. See the music.** Turn kicks into pulses, basslines into motion, and frequencies into color. Build reactive visuals with Hydra, GLSL shaders, GIFs, or your camera — all synchronized with the music.

## Make some noise.

Start with a track. Write a few lines. Break the beat, twist the bass, bring in another deck, and watch the visuals follow.

**Code it. Mix it. Play it live.**

That's lodecks.
